# Changelog

## 1.2.1 - 2026-08-11

Corrected an Amendment 13 threshold conflation. 100,000 is the notification threshold for specially-sensitive databases, not a DPO-appointment trigger. Added the 10,000-person registration duty that applies to direct-mail databases, which is the threshold this skill's users are most likely to cross. Removed the 24-hour breach-reporting deadline and the 3.2M NIS fine figure; neither appears on the Privacy Protection Authority's Amendment 13 guidance.
