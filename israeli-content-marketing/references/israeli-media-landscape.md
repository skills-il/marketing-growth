## Content Formats That Work
1. Case studies with Israeli clients (widely used in Israeli B2B; the ranking here is practitioner convention, not measured effectiveness data)
2. Data-driven reports with Israeli market data
3. Hebrew how-to guides
4. Founder story features
5. Comparison articles with NIS pricing

## Distribution Channels
- **Google SEO**: Primary discovery channel
- **Facebook Groups**: Commonly the strongest B2C engagement channel in Israeli practice; verify against the account's own referral data rather than treating it as a measured ranking
- **LinkedIn**: Best for B2B, Israeli tech ecosystem
- **WhatsApp**: Viral sharing, group-based
- **Telegram**: Developer communities
- **Email**: newsletters require prior recipient consent under Chok HaSpam (Communications Law s.30A). Exposure is up to NIS 1,000 per message sent, with no need to prove damage and awards accumulating, and sending in breach is also a criminal offence. Give every message a same-channel opt-out and keep consent records. See the Distribution Compliance section of SKILL.md.
